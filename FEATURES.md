# evynly — Features

> Split expenses effortlessly with friends, roommates, and travel buddies.

---

## Core Features (Free)

### Groups & Friends
- Create up to **2 groups** (e.g. roommates, trips, dinner)
- Add up to **4 members per group**
- Invite friends by searching their email address
- **Contact suggestions** pulled from your phone contacts
- Send & accept friend requests with a pending/received tab
- Manage member roles within a group

### Expense Tracking
- Add expenses with a description and amount
- **Equal split** among selected group members
- Up to **3 expenses per group** (15/month total)
- Expense categories visible on free tier
- Browse all expenses inside each group

### Settle Up
- **Optimized debt settlement** — minimizes the number of transactions needed to clear all balances
- View who owes who and by how much
- Mark settlements as paid with one tap
- Send and receive settlement requests

### Activity Feed
- Unified timeline of all expenses and settlements across every group
- Filter by **All**, **Expenses**, or **Settlements**
- See your involvement (you paid / you owe / you get back) at a glance

### Notifications
- Push notifications for new expenses, settlements, and friend requests
- **1 debt reminder per month** to nudge friends who owe you

### Onboarding
- Friendly onboarding flow for first-time users
- Step-by-step guide: create a group → add an expense → settle up

## Premium Features (One-time ₹149 / $1.99)

### No Limits
- **Unlimited groups** — no cap on how many you can create
- **Unlimited members** per group
- **Unlimited expenses** — add as many as you need, forever

### Advanced Splits
- Split by **percentage** (e.g. 60/40)
- Split by **exact amounts** (e.g. each person pays a specific figure)
- Split by **custom share ratios**

### Group Chat
- Real-time **in-group chat** to discuss expenses and coordinate payments
- Unlimited messages

### Analytics Dashboard
- Visual **spending breakdown by category** (food, transport, utilities, etc.)
- Monthly spending trends
- Per-person contribution summaries
- Identify your biggest expense areas at a glance

### Export Reports
- Export full group expense history as a **CSV file**
- Includes date, description, category, who paid, amount, per-person share
- Settlement summary and balance summary included

### Receipt Scanning
- **Scan a receipt** using your camera
- OCR automatically reads and pre-fills the description and amount
- Tips for best scan results built in

### Payment Proof
- Attach a **photo as proof of payment** when settling up
- View attached proof photos inside the activity feed

### Recurring Expenses
- Set up expenses that repeat **daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly**
- Perfect for rent, subscriptions, and regular bills
- Manage all recurring items from a dedicated screen

### Unlimited Reminders
- Send **unlimited payment reminders** to friends who owe you
- No monthly cap

### Multi-Currency Support
- Track expenses in **different currencies**
- Useful for international trips and groups spanning multiple countries

### Expense Reactions
- React to expenses with emoji reactions
- Keep the mood light when splitting the dinner bill

### Expense Notes
- Attach a note to any expense for extra context

### Budget Limits
- Set spending limits to keep group expenses in check

### Custom Group Icons
- Personalize each group with a custom icon

### Ad-Free Experience
- No banners, no interruptions — completely ad-free

### Priority Support
- Get faster help when something goes wrong

## Platform

- Available on **iOS** and **Android**
- Built with React Native (Expo) — fast, native performance on both platforms
- Powered by **Firebase** — real-time sync across all your devices
- Secure authentication via email/password
