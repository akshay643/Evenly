# Data Deletion — evynly

## How to Delete Your Account & Data

evynly gives you full control over your data. You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal information at any time through either of the methods below.

---

## Option 1 — Delete Directly In-App (Recommended)

1. Open the **evynly** app
2. Tap the **Profile** tab (bottom-right)
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **"Delete Account & Data"** under the *Danger Zone* section
4. Enter your password to confirm
5. Your account and personal data will be deleted immediately

This is the fastest way — deletion is instant and irreversible.

---

## Option 2 — Email Request

If you cannot access the app, use the web form below to submit a deletion request:

**[https://evynly.app/data-deletion](https://evynly.app/data-deletion)**

Or email **privacy@evynly.app** directly with:
- The email address linked to your evynly account
- Subject line: `Account Deletion Request`

We will process your request within **7 business days** and confirm deletion by email.

---

## What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently removed:

| Data | Deleted? |
|------|----------|
| Account credentials (email & password) | ✅ Yes — immediately |
| Profile info (name, photo) | ✅ Yes — immediately |
| Payment info (UPI ID, bank details) | ✅ Yes — immediately |
| Push notification tokens | ✅ Yes — immediately |
| Premium purchase record | ✅ Yes |

### Data That May Remain

Some data tied to shared activity is retained in anonymised or aggregate form:

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Expenses in groups you belonged to | Retained (your name replaced with "Deleted User") to preserve accurate balances for remaining members |
| Settlement records | Retained for group accounting integrity |

If you want all expense records removed as well, include this request in your email to **privacy@evynly.app** and we will oblige.

---

## Retention Period

All personal data is deleted within **30 days** of a confirmed deletion request.
